About the name

Davestia combines parts of David and Setiadhi into one word that is easy to say and spell in any language. It is a name for the research, not a separate institution: affiliations on papers are always the university and the company where the work is done.

Working together

I welcome joint research on ozone and plasma technology, CFD and FEM studies, and industry projects in mechanism and fixture design. Send an email.
