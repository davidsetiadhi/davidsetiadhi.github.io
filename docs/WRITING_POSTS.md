How to publish a post

1. Copy one of the drafts in this folder, or start a new file.
2. Rename it with the date in front, for example `2026-12-05-my-defense.md`, and move it to `_posts/`.
3. Push to GitHub. The site rebuilds in a few minutes and the post appears on /posts/ and on the About page.

Preview drafts locally with:
bundle exec jekyll serve --config _config.yml,_config.local.yml --drafts
