Coating and ballistic mechanics

Earlier FEM work at UMS. Residual stress in PVD TiN coatings and anti-projectile performance of armor

Finite element work from my years at Universitas Muhammadiyah Surakarta, with Prof. Tri Widodo Besar Riyadi:

  • PVD TiN coatings. Mechanical and thermal residual stresses that develop in a TiN coating on an iron substrate during physical vapor deposition (my M.Eng. thesis topic).
  • Ballistic impact. Anti-projectile performance of alumina ceramic and Weldox 460 E steel.
  • High-temperature creep. Creep of a Sanicro 25 boiler pipe under thermal load (my B.Eng. thesis topic).

Related work

  • Riyadi, Setiadhi, Anggono, Siswanto, Al-Kayiem (2021). Analysis of mechanical and thermal stresses due to TiN coating of Fe substrate by physical vapor deposition. Forces in Mechanics 4. doi:10.1016/j.finmec.2021.100042
  • Pratiwi, Riyadi, Setiadhi, Hariyanto (2023). Ballistic performance anti-projectile of alumina and Weldox 460 E with finite element method. Creative Research in Engineering 3(1). doi:10.30595/cerie.v3i1.15400
  • Riyadi, Setiadhi, Anggono, Darmawan (2018). Creep modeling of a Sanicro 25 steel pipe. Jurnal Teknik Mesin Indonesia 13(2).
