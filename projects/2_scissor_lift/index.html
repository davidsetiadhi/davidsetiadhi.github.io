Double scissor-lift for Bosch TS5 lines

Industrial R&D at Mertay Makina. Concept-to-delivery design of a conveyor-integrated lifting mechanism

A conveyor-integrated double scissor-lift mechanism developed at Mertay Makina for Bosch TS5 assembly lines. I led the development from concept to fabrication:

  • concept and mechanism layout,
  • structural analysis and safety-factor checks (ANSYS Workbench, Abaqus),
  • hydraulic sizing,
  • fabrication oversight and coordination with the client’s engineering team.

Two units were fabricated and delivered to the client.
