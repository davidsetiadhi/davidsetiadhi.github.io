Research

Research and industry projects, with the papers behind them.

research

  • Ozone sterilization chamber

    PhD research. Mathematical model, CFD, and experiments on ozone transport in a closed recirculating chamber

  • EV battery pack case thermal study

    Comparative FEM of sheet metal and SMC battery cases, with UN/ECE R100 thermal-shock testing

  • Coating and ballistic mechanics

    Earlier FEM work at UMS. Residual stress in PVD TiN coatings and anti-projectile performance of armor

  • Water injection in a small motorcycle engine

    Lab tests on a real single-cylinder engine, looking at how a higher compression ratio and a small spray of water work together. Paper in preparation.

  • Bullet impact on armor steel plates

    Computer simulation of bullets hitting standard military armor steels, checked against published tests, to compare plates before costly live firing.

  • Dental fillings that stay bonded

    A computer model of a filled premolar tooth under chewing and hot and cold cycles, fed by data checked against published studies.

  • Cleaning polluted soil with ozone

    A structured review of lab studies on ozone treatment of polluted soil, bringing their data into one checked dataset.

industry

  • Double scissor-lift for Bosch TS5 lines

    Industrial R&D at Mertay Makina. Concept-to-delivery design of a conveyor-integrated lifting mechanism

  • Maintenance stair platform stability

    Rigid-body dynamics study of the tipping threshold of undocked platforms

  • Choosing the steel for scissor lift arms

    How a machine maker can choose the cheapest steel for lift arms that is still safe and stiff enough. First paper in preparation.
