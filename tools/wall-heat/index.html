Heat flow through a layered wall

U-value, heat flow, and surface temperatures for one or two layers with convection.

Steady one-dimensional heat flow through a wall of up to two layers with convection on both sides, such as an enclosure panel with insulation or a battery case wall.

Figure 2. Temperature T (°C) through the solid layers against position from the inner surface (mm), for the values above. The dashed lines are the inside and outside fluid temperatures, and the gaps between them and the wall ends are the drops across the two convection films.

Limits

Steady state, flat wall, no radiation term, no contact resistance between layers. Transient cases such as a thermal-shock test need a time-dependent model.
