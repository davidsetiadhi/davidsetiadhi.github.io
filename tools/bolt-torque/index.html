Bolt preload and tightening torque

Metric bolts, ISO 898-1 property classes, and the nut-factor torque relation.

The torque that brings a metric bolt to a chosen share of its proof load, using the short-form torque relation.

Formulas

Fp=Sp At,Fi=f Fp,T=K Fi dF_p = S_p\, A_t, \qquad F_i = f\, F_p, \qquad T = K\, F_i\, dFp​=Sp​At​,Fi​=fFp​,T=KFi​d

with tensile stress area AtA_tAt​ and proof stress SpS_pSp​ from ISO 898-1 (class 8.8: 580 MPa up to M16 and 600 MPa above; 10.9: 830 MPa; 12.9: 970 MPa).

Limits

The nut factor scatters by ±25% or more with surface finish and lubrication, so the preload from a torque wrench scatters too. For critical joints, use the torque the fastener supplier gives or measure preload directly.

References

  • ISO 898-1, Mechanical properties of fasteners made of carbon steel and alloy steel.
  • Budynas, Nisbett, Shigley’s Mechanical Engineering Design, McGraw-Hill, chapter on screws and fasteners.
