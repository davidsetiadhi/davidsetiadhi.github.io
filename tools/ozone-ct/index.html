Ozone CT dose and exposure time

Exposure time for a target concentration × time dose, with ppm to mg/m³ conversion.

Ozone disinfection and sterilization are usually specified as a CT dose: concentration multiplied by contact time. This calculator turns a target dose into an exposure time and converts between volume (ppm) and mass (mg/m³) units at your gas temperature and pressure.

Figure 2. Accumulated dose C·t (ppm·min) against exposure time t (min) for the values above. The dashed line is the target CT, and the marked point is the exposure time that reaches it.

Formulas

t=CTC,Cmg/m3=Cppm MO3 pR Tt = \frac{CT}{C}, \qquad C_{\text{mg/m}^3} = \frac{C_{\text{ppm}}\, M_{\text{O}_3}\, p}{R\,T}t=CCT​,Cmg/m3​=RTCppm​MO3​​p​

with MO3=48.00M_{\text{O}_3} = 48.00MO3​​=48.00 g/mol and the ideal-gas molar volume RT/pRT/pRT/p (24.47 L/mol at 25 °C and 1 atm, so 1 ppm ≈ 1.96 mg/m³).

Limits

The CT a process needs depends on the organism, the relative humidity, the load, and the standard you validate against. This tool does the arithmetic only. It does not tell you which CT to use.
