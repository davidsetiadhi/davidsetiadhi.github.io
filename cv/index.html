Selected projects

  • Ozone sterilization chamber (PhD): a mathematical model and ANSYS Fluent CFD of ozone generation and transport in a closed recirculating chamber, checked against experiments on a prototype I built.

  • EV battery pack case thermal study (TÜBİTAK 1501 funded): FEM comparison of sheet metal and SMC cases, validated against analytical conduction and convection solutions, with thermal-shock testing to UN/ECE Regulation No. 100.
