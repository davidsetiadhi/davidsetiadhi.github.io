David Setiadhi

Mechanical engineer in Bursa, Türkiye

I build computer models and lab tests to answer practical engineering questions, such as whether every tool inside a sterilizer gets enough ozone, or which steel is the cheapest safe choice for a machine part.

I am finishing my PhD at Bursa Uludağ University, and I work as an R&D engineer at Mertay Makina, where I design machines for factory lines.

Available from January 2027. PhD defense in mid-December 2026. Open to postdoc, lecturer and R&D engineer roles in Türkiye, Indonesia or abroad.

Five areas, one way of working

I build a model of a real problem, check it against measurements, and turn it into an answer someone can use. Pick an area to see who it helps and where it is going.

Environment

Why it matters
Cleaning up soil polluted by oil, fuel and farm chemicals.
Where it is going
Finish checking the data from many lab studies so their results can be compared on one scale, then publish.

Finish the armor simulations, so that plate options can be compared before costly live firing tests.

Dental fillings

Ongoing2026 to now

A filling can come loose or crack when its bond to the tooth is weak. I am building a computer model of a filled tooth, fed by data checked against published studies, so that options can be compared without testing on real teeth.

My role
Leads the project. Designed the study, builds the simulation model, and set up how the data is collected and checked.
Tools and skills
ANSYS Workbench, SolidWorks, Python, Systematic review
Read about this project

Ozone sterilizer

Ongoing2022 to now · PhD

Hospitals clean heat-sensitive tools with ozone gas, but a single sensor reads the dose at one spot only. I study how the gas moves and breaks down inside the chamber, so that every surface gets enough.

My role
Designed and built the prototype chamber, ran the tests, wrote the models and ran the computer simulations (CFD).
Tools and skills
ANSYS Fluent CFD, Python, Prototype build, Sensors and data logging
  • PDProf. Dr. Kadir ÇavdarPhD supervisor
Read about this project

Ozone soil cleanup

Ongoing2026 to now

Polluted soil is slow and costly to clean, and lab studies on ozone treatment are hard to compare. I lead a structured review of those studies and bring their data into one checked dataset.

My role
Leads the project. Set the research questions and designed the review and the database.
Tools and skills
Systematic review (PRISMA), Database design, Python, Data quality control
Read about this project

Engine water injection

Ongoing2025 to now

Small motorcycle engines lose part of their fuel as heat. With a partner lab in Indonesia, we tested on a real engine how two simple changes, a higher compression ratio and a small spray of water, work together.

My role
Corresponding author. Ran the statistical analysis, made the figures, wrote the paper and released the data.
Tools and skills
Python statistics, Design of experiments, Engine testing, Open data
Read about this project

Choosing lift steel

Ongoing2025 to now

The steel for lift arms is often chosen by strength first and cost later. This study shows how a machine maker can choose the cheapest steel that is still safe and stiff enough.

My role
Leads the project. Ran the simulations, built the calculation and cost models, and is writing the paper.
Tools and skills
ANSYS Mechanical, MATLAB, Cost comparison, Eurocode
Read about this project

Stair platform safety

Published2025

Mobile maintenance stairs can tip over when they are not docked. This study finds the load at which that happens, as a basis for safe design limits.

My role
First author of the conference paper (2025).
Tools and skills
Rigid-body dynamics, Stability analysis
Read about this project

Armor steel impact

OngoingOngoing

Choosing an armor plate usually takes many costly firing tests. We simulate bullets hitting standard military armor steels, checked against published tests, to compare plates before live testing.

My role
Leads the project. Built the material models, runs the impact simulations and wrote the analysis scripts.
Tools and skills
LS-DYNA, High-speed impact FEM, Material model calibration, MATLAB
Read about this project

Coatings and armor (UMS)

Published2018 to 2023

Earlier finite element work at Universitas Muhammadiyah Surakarta. Stress inside hard TiN coatings, armor against projectiles, and creep in boiler pipes.

My role
Co-author. The TiN coating study was my M.Eng. thesis.
Tools and skills
Abaqus, ANSYS, Residual stress, Ballistic FEM
