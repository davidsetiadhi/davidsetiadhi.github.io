David Setiadhi

PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering, Bursa Uludağ University · R&D Engineer, Mertay Makina · Bursa, Türkiye

Bursa, Türkiye

I am a mechanical engineer and PhD candidate at Bursa Uludağ University (defense expected December 2026), combining doctoral research in CFD, mathematical modeling, and experimental validation with 3.5 years of industrial R&D.

My dissertation studies gas-phase ozone transport in a closed recirculating sterilization chamber for reusable medical devices. I couple a mathematical model of ozone generation and decay with ANSYS Fluent simulation, and validate both against measurements on an experimental setup I built and instrumented. The technical background of this work is published as a review in the Journal of Physics D: Applied Physics (2026).

In industry, I work as an R&D Engineer at Mertay Makina in Bursa, designing and structurally validating precision fixtures, jigs, and mechanisms for automotive and industrial clients including Bosch, Tesla (Gigafactory Berlin), Odelo, and OPmobility. Before moving to Türkiye, I spent several years at Universitas Muhammadiyah Surakarta (UMS), Indonesia, as a research and teaching assistant in finite element analysis, where I also earned my B.Eng. and M.Eng. in mechanical engineering.

Research interests

  • Ozone and plasma-based sterilization systems for medical devices
  • CFD and predictive modeling of gas-phase transport and reaction
  • Engineering optimization of sterilization processes and equipment
  • Thermal and structural FEM, from coatings and armor to EV battery cases
  • Precision mechanism design and validation for industrial automation
